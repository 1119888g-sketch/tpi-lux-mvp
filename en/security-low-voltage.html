Scope of work

What this service covers

The outline is drawn from working material and presented here in the format of a commercial page.

Video surveillance

Outdoor and indoor cameras, recording and data storage, remote monitoring and video analytics.

Surveillance installation

Camera mounting, cable laying, recording setup and remote access configuration.

Security design

Site analysis, equipment layout drawings, documentation and choice of system architecture.

Access control

Turnstiles, electronic locks, readers, controllers and entry logging systems.

Low-voltage networks

Structured cabling systems, data networks, communication lines and signal distribution.

Integration and service

Bringing surveillance and access together, notifications, monitoring, diagnostics and maintenance.
