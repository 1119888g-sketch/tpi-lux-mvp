LED garlands and light figures

We create lighting structures for streets, squares, facades, parks and events: from equipment selection through to installation, scenes, removal and seasonal storage.

Discuss a project See scope of work
The TPI LUX principle Engineering, not just decoration

The approach is engineering-led, not merely decorative: every structure has to look striking, operate safely and stand up to real-world use.

Scope of work

What this service covers

The outline is drawn from working material and presented here in the format of a commercial page.

Matched to the site

We calculate the number of elements, the brightness and the types of garland and structure for the task, the scale and the budget.

Manufacturing and fabrication

We fabricate light figures, bespoke structures and decorative elements under quality control.

Installation and connection

We install structures of any complexity, connect them, test them and meet safety requirements.

Lighting control

We set up static and dynamic scenes, effect synchronisation and integration with controllers.

Removal and storage

We remove the equipment carefully, arrange storage and prepare it for reinstallation.

Service

Scheduled servicing, repair, replacement of components and support throughout operation.
