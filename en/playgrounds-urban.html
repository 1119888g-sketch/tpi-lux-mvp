Scope of work

What this service covers

The outline is drawn from working material and presented here in the format of a commercial page.

Playgrounds

Play complexes, slides, towers, swings, roundabouts, rope elements and climbing frames.

Zone design

Site analysis, activity planning, equipment selection and design documentation.

Play elements

Seesaws, spring rockers, sandpits, play panels and individual small structures.

Manufacturing of structures

Metal structures, decorative elements, equipment preparation and quality control.

Park furniture

Benches, seating, bins, tables, rest areas and decorative public realm elements.

Installation and maintenance

Base preparation, installation, fixings, safety checks, inspections and repair of elements.
